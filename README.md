# Green-nephrology
Feasibility Study and Technical Design of a Water Reuse System — Case Study of a Hemodialysis Center in Agadir, Morocco
# Valorization of Reverse Osmosis Reject Water in Hemodialysis

## Overview

This project was developed as part of a Professional Bachelor's
internship carried out between March and June 2022.

The study investigates the reuse and valorization of reject water
generated by a reverse osmosis system used in a hemodialysis center.

## Objectives

- Quantify the reject-water flow generated by the RO system
- Identify suitable non-medical reuse applications
- Study partial recycling upstream of the RO system
- Evaluate the feasibility of pressure/hydraulic energy recovery
- Design a prototype monitoring and control system
- Estimate the economic potential of the proposed solutions

## Field measurements

The main operating data used in this study were collected directly
on site during the internship.

Measured average reject flow:

**2,400 L/h**

Equivalent 24-hour extrapolation:

**57,600 L/day**

> The values above are field measurements and extrapolations
> specific to the studied installation.
